About us

Good study help should be free

Description
Easified is a free study app for South African learners in Grades 10 to 12. It has CAPS-aligned lessons, diagrams, worked examples, quizzes, past papers and a built-in scientific calculator, in all 11 official languages. It has no adverts and never sells anyone's data.
Founder
Ntsako Khosa, founder of EASIFIEDCORP (Pty) Ltd.
Location
South Africa
Founded
1 October 2026 · CIPC registration 2026/786643/07

Goals & aims

  • Make quality CAPS study material free for every learner, whatever their school or background.
  • Help more learners pass matric, and pass it well.
  • Give teachers simple tools to track their classes, set homework and support learners.
  • Teach in the learner's own language so that nobody is left behind.

Why it matters

Many learners can't afford tutors or textbooks. Easified puts a study guide, practice papers and a calculator in every learner's pocket, for free. It helps narrow the gap between well-resourced and under-resourced schools and gives young South Africans a fairer chance at their future.

Meet the founder
